I am an Associate Professor in the mathematics department at Lehigh University. I was a PhD student at the University of Alberta from 2011-2016 and I was a post-doc in the Simons Collaboration in HMS from 2016-2018.

I study problems in algebraic geometry. More precisely, I work with Calabi-Yau varieties and their relations to mirror symmetry and Hodge theory.
